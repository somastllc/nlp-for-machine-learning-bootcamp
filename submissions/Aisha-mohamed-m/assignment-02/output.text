5) STOPWORDS AND NEGATION
======================================================================
T2 tokens:
['I', 'do', "n't", 'think', 'this', 'jacket', 'is', 'worth', 'the', 'price', '.', 'Not', 'happy', 'at', 'all', '😞']

Default NLTK stopword removal:
["n't", 'think', 'jacket', 'worth', 'price', '.', 'happy', '😞']

Custom stopword removal:
Keeping: not, n't, no, never
["n't", 'think', 'jacket', 'worth', 'price', '.', 'Not', 'happy', '😞']

Why?
Negation words can change sentiment and meaning. For example, 'happy' and 'not happy' have opposite meanings. Removing 'not' can therefore damage a sentiment or complaint classifier.

FINAL SUMMARY
======================================================================
Preprocessing is task-dependent. Lowercasing and whitespace normalization can reduce unnecessary variation, but aggressive regex cleaning or stopword removal can delete useful information.
For spam detection, preserve useful signals such as phone numbers, money amounts, capitalization, and repeated punctuation when appropriate.
For sentiment or complaint detection, preserve negation words such as 'not', 'no', 'never', and important forms of 'n't'.
