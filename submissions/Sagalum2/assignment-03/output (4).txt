==============================================================================
Vocabulary (size 6):

Count vectors:

R2            1          1          0          0          1          1
R3            0          1          0          1          1          1

Cosine similarity matrix:
        R1    R2    R3
R1     1.0  0.75  0.75
R2    0.75   1.0  0.75
R3    0.75  0.75   1.0

sim(R1, R2) [tasty vs delicious] = 0.750

Answer: no. Each review shares 3 of its 4 words with the others, and 'tasty',

know that tasty and delicious are close in meaning while terrible is the opposite.
